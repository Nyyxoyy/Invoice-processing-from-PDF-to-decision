How this works

Drop a vendor invoice PDF. The pipeline reads it, verifies every value against the document, matches it to a purchase order, and makes one of four decisions:

Auto-approved — everything verified, within budget. The amount posts to the PO ledger.
Approved · exception — a small overage inside the authorized band. Posts, and the exception is logged.
Held for review — something is uncertain or missing. Nothing posts until a person resolves it on the run page.
Rejected — a certain violation (duplicate, blocked vendor). Final.

The core rule: the AI only reads — code decides all money. Every decision shows its reasons, its arithmetic, and a full step-by-step trace.
